Privacy Policy

KOLABA web test version · Effective 8 November 2026

Who is responsible

KOLABA is operated by Milka Yevhenii, Ukraine. For questions about your personal data or to exercise your rights, contact mlkdesignweb@gmail.com.

What KOLABA does

KOLABA connects creators and businesses through profiles, portfolios, opportunities, applications and messages. This is a public test service. Demonstration profiles are fictional. Test data may be reset; keep your own copies of important content.

Data we process and why

  • Account and sign-in: email address, account identifier, verification status and security records, to create and protect your account. Where password sign-in is available, we store a salted password hash rather than the password itself.
  • Profile and activity: the names, biography, location, links, portfolio, opportunities, applications, contacts, reviews and other information you choose to provide, to operate the features you use.
  • Communications: messages, attachments, delivery and read status, notifications and reports, to support communication and moderation.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser information, session records, request and error logs, to deliver the website, prevent abuse and investigate faults.

Sign in with Google

Google sign-in is being prepared and is not yet available in the live application. When enabled and selected by you, it will use your Google account identifier, email and basic profile information to authenticate you and create or link your KOLABA account. It will not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or calendars. KOLABA does not receive your Google password. Google processes sign-in activity under its own Privacy Policy. This notice must be updated if the actual integration changes.

Who can see your information

Public profile fields, portfolios, published opportunities and reviews are visible to other users and may be accessible to visitors. Applications and conversations are provided to their intended recipients. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. The operator and authorised service providers may process data to operate, secure and moderate the service. Do not post sensitive information or content you cannot lawfully share.

Service providers and external content

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Cloudflare provides the API, database, network security and, when enabled, media storage. Email verification and password-reset delivery use Resend when configured. Google provides the optional sign-in service when enabled. Requests to externally hosted demonstration images or videos can reveal technical information, such as your IP address, to their hosts. External sites you choose to visit have their own privacy policies.

These providers operate international infrastructure, so processing may occur outside Ukraine or your country. For information about the providers involved in a particular request or the transfer arrangements applicable to your data, contact the operator.

Usage analytics (Microsoft Clarity)

To understand how KOLABA is used and fix problems, the website uses Microsoft Clarity, which records anonymised interactions such as screens visited, taps, clicks and scrolling. Passwords, verification codes, account forms, messages, notifications and other private areas are masked and are not captured as text. KOLABA does not send your name, email or account identifier to Clarity. Clarity sets its own cookies and processes data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Analytics are switched off if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Safety → Usage analytics.

Browser storage

The application uses browser local storage to remember your session, selected profile, invitation information and your usage-analytics choice. Signing out removes the active sign-in information used by the application. You can also clear site storage through your browser; doing so does not delete records held on the server.

Basis and retention

We process information needed to provide the service you request, protect it against misuse, meet applicable legal obligations and, where required, act on your consent. Where applicable law requires a particular legal basis, that basis applies to the corresponding processing.

Account and user content currently have no automatic deletion deadline. They remain until manually deleted following a request, moderation action or test reset. You may request account closure and deletion by email. Any retention after a deletion request must be limited to what is necessary for security, resolving a dispute or a legal obligation. Infrastructure logs and recovery copies are subject to the providers’ configured retention periods and may remain temporarily after active data is deleted. Copies that other users have independently made cannot necessarily be withdrawn.

Your choices and rights

You can edit available profile fields and visibility settings. Contact the operator to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your information. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, receive portable data, withdraw consent and complain to the competent data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Updates

We will update this page when processing changes. Material changes should be brought to users’ attention before they take effect.
