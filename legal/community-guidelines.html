Community Guidelines

Effective 10 November 2026 · applies to the kolaba iOS app and the kolaba website

kolaba is a professional space for creators and businesses. These rules apply to profiles, photos and videos, job posts, applications, messages and reviews. They are part of the Terms of Service.

Not allowed

  • Sexual content — pornography, nudity, sexual services, and any sexualisation or exploitation of minors (reported to the authorities).
  • Violence and threats — threats, glorifying violence, self-harm encouragement, dangerous acts.
  • Harassment and bullying — insults, intimidation, unwanted repeated contact, sexual harassment.
  • Hate — attacks on people for race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar characteristics.
  • Scams and fraud — fake jobs, advance-fee or “pay to apply” schemes, phishing for passwords or codes, fake payments.
  • Spam — mass or repetitive messages, unrelated advertising, link farming.
  • Fake accounts and impersonation — pretending to be another person or brand, misleading profiles.
  • Illegal content and goods — drugs, weapons, stolen data, anything illegal where you or the recipient are.
  • Copyright and IP infringement — posting work, music, footage or brand assets you have no rights to.
  • Other people’s personal data — publishing someone’s contact details, documents or private information without consent.
  • Review manipulation — fake reviews, reviewing yourself, trading or buying reviews, pressuring someone over a review.

How to report

Use Report on a profile (••• menu), a job post (••• menu), a photo or video (long press), a message (long press), a conversation (••• in the chat) or a review. Choose a reason and add details if you like. Reports are confidential: the person you report never sees who reported them. When you report messages, you choose to share the reported message and a few messages before it with our moderators. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

How to block

Use Block on a person’s profile or in your chat with them. Blocked people can’t message you, send contact requests, apply to your jobs, review you or appear in your feeds, and you get no notifications from them. Your existing chat stays. Unblock any time in Settings → Privacy & Safety → Blocked users.

How moderation works

  • New public photos and videos are checked by a person before others can see them (“Pending review” in your profile until then).
  • Automatic rules flag possible spam, scams, threats and explicit text for a person to check. Automated checks alone never ban anyone.
  • Reports are reviewed by the kolaba team, urgent ones (threats, illegal content) first.

What can happen

Depending on how serious and repeated a violation is: the content is removed; a warning; a temporary restriction (you can still sign in but can’t post, message or apply); account suspension; a permanent ban. Illegal content may be reported to the authorities.

Appeals

If you think we got it wrong, write to mlkdesignweb@gmail.com from your account’s email within 30 days, with what was removed or restricted and why you disagree. We will look at the decision again and answer you.
