Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · applies to the kolaba iOS app and the kolaba website

1. Who is responsible

kolaba (the iOS app and the website at mlkdesign.github.io/kolaba) is operated by Milka Yevhenii, Ukraine (“we”). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for anything about your data: mlkdesignweb@gmail.com.

2. What kolaba is

kolaba connects content creators (UGC creators) and businesses. Creators publish a profile and a portfolio of photos and videos; businesses publish job posts; creators apply; people add each other as contacts, message each other and leave reviews. Paid plans are not offered: kolaba is currently free and nothing can be bought in the app.

4. Device permissions (iOS)

  • Photos — only when you choose photos or videos to upload or send.
  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo or video to send.
  • Microphone — only while you record a voice message.
  • Location — only when you tap “Use my location” to fill in a job’s creator location; we keep only the city and country you confirm.

Your sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain. Some preferences (such as haptics and language) stay only on your device.

5. Who can see your information

  • Public: profiles, approved portfolio media, published job posts and approved reviews are visible to other kolaba users.
  • Private: applications are visible to you and the business that posted the job; messages are visible to the two participants.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on our servers, where they can technically be read. We do not read conversations. Moderators see messages only when a participant reports them and agrees to share them, and only the copy shared with the report. Every such access is logged.
  • Moderators review new public photos and videos before they become visible to others, and review reported content.

6. Moderation and automated checks

New public photos and videos wait for a person to approve them. Public texts (job posts, profile texts, reviews) are checked by our own automatic rules for spam, scams, threats and explicit content; a match only sends the item to a person for review — automated checks never ban anyone. We do not currently send your content to any third-party content-classification service; if we start to, we will update this policy first.

7. Service providers

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — servers, database, file storage, network security (all kolaba data).
  • GitHub, Inc. — hosting of the kolaba website and these pages.
  • Resend — sending verification and password-reset emails (your email address and the code).
  • Google — only if you choose “Sign in with Google” on the website (your Google account ID, email and name).
  • BigDataCloud — only on the website, when you tap “Use my location” (rounded coordinates to find your city).
  • Microsoft Clarity — only on the website, if you leave usage analytics on (anonymised interactions; private areas are masked). It is not active in the iOS app.
  • Apple — distributes the iOS app; Apple’s own policies apply to the App Store.

These providers process data for us under their terms. Some of them process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the GDPR applies, such transfers rely on the providers’ Standard Contractual Clauses or other transfer mechanisms they offer. Ask us for details.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account, profiles, media, jobs, applications, messages, reviews: until you delete them or your account.
  • When you delete your account, we immediately delete your account, all your profiles, your photos, videos and voice messages (including the files), your jobs and applications, the messages you sent, reviews you wrote or received, your contacts, likes, blocks, settings and feedback, and end all your sessions.
  • Exceptions: reports you filed stay with our moderators without your name; copies of messages shared with a report are deleted 180 days after the report is closed; if an account was permanently banned for breaking our rules, we keep a one-way hash of its email address so it can’t be used to sign up again; our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for up to 30 days.
  • Technical logs: kept by our providers for their standard periods (typically days to weeks).

9. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account (you confirm with your password). Deleting one business profile (Settings → Account) keeps your account and your other profiles. You can also ask us by email.

10. Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data in the app, and ask us for a copy of your data, to restrict or object to processing, or to delete data you can’t delete yourself. If the GDPR or a similar law applies to you, you also have the right to data portability and to complain to your data-protection authority. Write to mlkdesignweb@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity.

11. Children

kolaba is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child uses kolaba, tell us and we will delete the account.

12. Changes

We will update this page when our processing changes and tell you in the app about significant changes before they take effect.
