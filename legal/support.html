Support

Effective 10 November 2026 · applies to the kolaba iOS app and the kolaba website

Contact us

Email mlkdesignweb@gmail.com.

In the app you can also use Settings → Feedback & Suggestions to send an idea or report a bug (with an optional screenshot).

Report abuse

To report a person or their content, use Report in the app (on a profile, job post, photo or video, message, conversation or review). For urgent safety issues, also write to us. See the Community Guidelines.

Account and data

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Everything is deleted as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Delete one business profile: switch to it, then Settings → Account → Delete this business profile.
  • Blocked users: Settings → Privacy & Safety → Blocked users.
  • A copy of your data, corrections, other privacy requests: email us.

Copyright complaints

See section 7 of the Terms of Service and send your notice to mlkdesignweb@gmail.com.

Appeals

If content was removed or your account was restricted and you disagree, see “Appeals” in the Community Guidelines.
